# NovaCart business review

Synthetic portfolio case study · 2024-02-15 through 2025-12-31

## Executive brief

NovaCart booked **$1,378,385.95** in net revenue on **2,500 orders**.
Gross profit was **$600,965.30**. Revenue subtracts refunds from post-discount
sales. Experiment conversions come from the independent session dataset.

## Sales

The highest net-revenue month was 2025-11 at $172,279.39.
The final month, 2025-12, ended at $132,801.43.
Compare orders and AOV by week and check the data cutoff before attributing that change to demand.

## Regional performance

Midwest led with $395,051.80 from 704 orders.
Compare revenue per active customer and acquisition mix before shifting campaign spend.

## Checkout experiment

Treatment converted 11.52% of sessions versus
10.51% for control. The estimated difference was
1.01 percentage points, with an approximate 95% interval
from -0.57 to 2.60
percentage points. The two-sided p-value was 0.2100.
The test did not detect a difference at the 5% level. Before a rollout decision, define a minimum practical
lift and add refund and order-value guardrails.

## Decision review

Review the three findings with the scenario owner. Confirm completeness, assign an owner
to investigate month-end coverage, and agree on success and rollback criteria for the
checkout experiment. See bi/case-study/Acceptance.md for requirements and UAT steps.
